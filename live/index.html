Help fill the live views

The more observers in Indiana, the more of the state shows up live. An observer is a repeater or radio connected to a computer that uploads what it hears.

  1. Use meshcoretomqtt on a Raspberry Pi or computer connected to a MeshCore repeater. openHop repeaters have the same uploads built in.
  2. Any of the services works; Waev (setup) is listed first here for its documented privacy model, and OKI CoreScope, LetsMesh and MeshMapper are just as valid. Observers can feed several brokers at once.
  3. Say hi in the Indiana Mesh Discord so others know where coverage is.

These community networks are where Indiana's live traffic is.
