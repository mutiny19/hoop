#meshnet

A ten-minute check-in net on MeshCore, open to anyone in Indiana. It works like a weekly ham or GMRS net, with no net control: you post one message, and everyone within reach sees who's on the air.

Logged automatically on Sundays and Thursdays only, from 6:57 to 7:13 PM (the net plus three minutes each side); nothing outside that window is recorded. The log reads the #meshnet messages community observers upload to OKI Mesh's CoreScope and Chicagoland Mesh's CoreScope, one check-in per person. No names or callsigns are kept. A check-in shows here only if an observer heard it, so more observers means a fuller map. Meshtastic check-ins are not logged.

Why check in

  • Test your radio and your settings.
  • See who your messages reach, and who reaches you.
  • Practise, so it's routine when the mesh is needed.
  • Meet the people on the mesh near you.
  • Stress-test the mesh on purpose. Everyone posting at once is how weak spots show up.

If there is severe weather or any emergency on the mesh, the net is postponed. Emergency traffic always comes first.

How to check in

  1. In the MeshCore app, add the hashtag channel #meshnet, all lowercase. New to MeshCore? Start here.
  2. Between 7:00 and 7:10 PM, post one message with three things, separated by commas:

    KUMP, Heltec V3, 4 months

    Airport
    Your closest airport's four-letter code, starting with K. It doesn't need to be a big one; the nearest small field is better. Find yours on the Indiana airport map.
    Device
    The radio you're sending from, such as Heltec V3, T-Deck or Wio L1.
    Time on the mesh
    How long you've been on MeshCore: 4 months, 1 year, or new.
  3. Read the channel to see who else checked in. One message each keeps the channel clear.

Help it grow. Invite others the usual ways: tell a neighbour, mention it in your local group's chat or Discord, announce it on your local ham or GMRS net, or share this page. Every new check-in shows how far the mesh reaches.

When something fails

Finding failures is the point of the net. Each one tells you what the mesh needs next.

Nobody heard you
Your area needs a repeater with height and a good antenna. Put one up (the MeshCore card in Settings has a Repeater tab), or offer a high site to someone who will.
You got out, but you're not on the map
No observer heard you. Add one that feeds a public network.
Your observer feeds a different CoreScope
Let us know on GitHub or the Indiana Mesh Discord, and it can be added to the log.
